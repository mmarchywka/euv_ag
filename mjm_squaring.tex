consider a function I(x) between zero and one and a system that
can threshold at .5. Raising to a power and thresholding at
.5 for that power gives a derivative at threshold of

\mjmeqn{ \frac{d(I^n)}{dx} = nI^{n-1}\frac{dI}{dx} ; I^n=.5}


\mjmeqn{ \frac{d(I^n)}{dx} = n2^{(1-n)/n}\frac{dI}{dx}(I=.5^{1/n} )}
